Mutual Fund Categories

Know the basics before selecting a scheme.

📈

Large Cap

Primarily invests in established large companies and offers large-company equity exposure.

🚀

Mid Cap

Primarily invests in mid-sized companies and can have higher volatility than large-cap funds.

🌱

Small Cap

Primarily invests in smaller companies; higher growth potential can come with higher risk.

⚖️

Flexi Cap

Can invest across market capitalisations according to the scheme's strategy.

🏦

Debt Funds

Invest mainly in fixed-income securities; risks depend on the portfolio and market conditions.

🔄

Hybrid Funds

Combine equity and debt exposure according to the scheme's investment strategy.
